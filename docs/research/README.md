## Standing caution

Several citations in the memos are flagged as recalled rather than verified against the source. Nothing from this research should appear in marketing copy as a research claim without checking the verification section of the originating memo first. Invariant SAFE-2 is the hard line: no copy anywhere claims general memory improvement, because the best-controlled study of whole-text memorizers found no far transfer.
